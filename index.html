About

My path here wasn't a straight line, but there's one thread through it: creating structure in high-volume, detail-heavy environments.

I started as a science teacher, managing instruction, materials, and student records for a full course load. Before moving into content, I supported a community development monitoring and evaluation program for the Provincial Government of Agusan del Sur, helping refine a data-tracking tool and turning raw program data into reports stakeholders could act on.

Both built the same muscle I now use daily in content operations. For five months, I used AI tools to produce SEO content across two concurrent part-time roles, personally reviewing, editing, and publishing 2,400+ articles combined at a pace of 16 a day. From there, I moved into managing content operations, building the editorial QA system a 6-7 person team relies on to keep a 450+ article a month pipeline error-free and on-brand, and handling technical SEO work including content consolidation and search index management.
